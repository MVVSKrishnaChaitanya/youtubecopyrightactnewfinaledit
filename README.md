 Creator Studio & Copyright Shield 🎬This local tool scans your .mp4 video files before you upload them. It catches copyright mistakes, loud audio distortion, and platform layout issues right from your computer.🛡️ What It Does1. Copyright Shield Layer🎵 Music Registry: Checks your video audio against a list of your safe, licensed tracks (safe_assets_registry.csv).🔍 Risk Tracker: Finds accidental commercial placeholder files (.mp3/.wav) left in your editing project folder.🏷️ Metadata Scanner: Flags words in your file name (like crack, free, download) that trigger system bans.2. Video Optimization Layer📱 Layout Detector: Tells you if your video fits widescreen (16:9), vertical (9:16), or premium 4K standards.⏱️ The 60s Shorts Filter: Flags vertical videos over 60 seconds before the algorithm rejects them.🔊 Audio Peak Master: Catches ear-piercing sound distortion or dead silence that hurts viewer retention.🛠️ InstallationRun this command in your computer's terminal:bashpip install moviepy numpy
